# Google Play Store Listing — FlowTV

## App Name (max 30 chars)
FlowTV - IPTV Player

## Short Description (max 80 chars)
Stream IPTV channels with EPG guide, favorites, Chromecast, and multi-view.

## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
FlowTV is a free, open-source IPTV player that lets you watch your own IPTV channels on Android. Simply add your M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login and start streaming.

Features:

Channel Browsing
Browse and search through your IPTV channel list with category filtering. Channels load instantly with logos and group organization from your playlist.

TV Guide (EPG)
View a full electronic program guide showing what's on now and upcoming programs. The EPG loads from XMLTV sources configured in your playlist and auto-refreshes on a schedule you choose.

Favorites
Mark your most-watched channels as favorites for quick access from a dedicated tab.

Chromecast Support
Cast any channel to your Chromecast or Google Cast-compatible device directly from the channel list.

Secure Credentials
Xtream Codes login credentials are stored using Android Keystore encryption, never in plaintext.

Dark Theme
Clean, modern dark interface designed for comfortable viewing.

Playlist Support
- M3U / M3U8 playlists via URL or local file
- Xtream Codes API with username/password login
- Multiple playlists supported simultaneously

No Data Collection
FlowTV does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. All data stays on your device.

Open Source
FlowTV is licensed under GPL v3. The full source code is available on GitHub.

Note: FlowTV is a media player only. It does not provide, host, or include any streaming content. You must supply your own IPTV service credentials or playlist URLs.
